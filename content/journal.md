# 🚲 Tour Journal: Bristol to Flanders & Holland (April 4 – 15, 2024)

## 📌 Tour Overview
- **Riders:** Me (Penry), Mike & Ronaldo
- **Route:** Bristol ➔ London ➔ Dover (Train) ➔ Dunkirk (Ferry) ➔ Bruges ➔ Antwerp ➔ Diest ➔ Eindhoven ➔ Nijmegen ➔ Arnhem ➔ Utrecht (Train) ➔ Gouda ➔ Delft ➔ Zeeland ➔ Bruges ➔ Dunkirk ➔ Dover (Ferry) ➔ Bristol (Train)
- **Data:** Recorded on a phone with Google Fit, which missed a lot of the riding. The gaps are filled with routes along cycle paths between the points that *were* recorded. "Show raw GPS" on the map shows the original recording.

### **Wednesday, 10 April 2024**
* **Route:** Overasselt ➔ Arnhem ➔ Utrecht (Train) ➔ Breukelen ➔ Utrecht (Train, evening)
* **Log:** Took the train from Arnhem Centraal to Utrecht, then cycled on to Breukelen and the campsite nearby. After pitching camp we cycled back to Breukelen station and took the train into Utrecht for food and beers, then the train back and a ride to the campsite.
* **Overnight:** Camping Oukoop, near Breukelen.

---

### **Thursday, 11 April 2024**
* **Route:** Breukelen ➔ Gouda (Train) ➔ Delft ➔ Rotterdam (Train, evening)
* **Log:** Cycled to the station and took the train to Gouda for the cheese museum tour, then rode on to Delft. Train into Rotterdam for the evening and back.
* **Overnight:** Boat Airbnb, Delft.
